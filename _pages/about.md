I am a fourth-year undergraduate student majoring in biochemistry and microbiology at Kyoto University, Japan. I am currently conducting research on C1 metabolism in actinomycetes in the [Laboratory of Microbial Biotechnology](http://www.seigyo.kais.kyoto-u.ac.jp/). Inspired by the transformative impact of thermophilic DNA polymerases on PCR technology, I aspire to develop **novel biotechnologies based on proteins derived from extremophilic microorganisms**. I am particularly interested in understanding how proteins adapt to extreme environments and translating those principles into practical tools for biotechnology.
